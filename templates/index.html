About Us

Rajalakshmi Institutions, established in 1997, has emerged as a premier institution offering quality technical education. With state-of-the-art infrastructure and experienced faculty, we are committed to nurturing future leaders and innovators.

Our institution offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programs in engineering, management and computer applications. We focus on holistic development through academic excellence, research initiatives and industry collaborations.

The campus spread over 45 acres provides an ideal environment for learning and growth with modern laboratories, libraries, sports facilities and hostel accommodations.

Academics

Engineering

B.Tech programs in Computer Science, Mechanical, Civil, ECE, EEE and more with industry-oriented curriculum and hands-on learning.

Management

MBA program with specializations in Marketing, Finance, HR and Operations, focusing on developing business leaders.

Computer Applications

BCA and MCA programs designed to build strong foundation in computer applications and software development.
